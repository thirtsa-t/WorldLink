Air Freight Service

Our air freight service delivers your time-sensitive cargo with unmatched speed and reliability. With partnerships across major global airlines and dedicated cargo carriers, we ensure your shipments reach any destination worldwide within 24–72 hours. Perfect for urgent documents, high-value goods, and perishable items requiring rapid transport. Our real-time tracking system keeps you informed at every stage of the journey.

Why Choose Our Air Freight
  • 24–72 hour delivery worldwide
  • Real-time tracking and status updates
  • Special handling for perishable goods
  • Full customs clearance assistance
  • Competitive rates for urgent shipments
  • Secure packaging for high-value cargo

Ocean Freight Service

Our ocean freight solutions offer cost-effective transport for bulk cargo, heavy machinery, and containerized goods. We handle FCL (Full Container Load) and LCL (Less than Container Load) shipments to major ports worldwide. With flexible scheduling and comprehensive customs clearance support, we make international sea trade simple and efficient. Choose from standard, expedited, or refrigerated container options.

Why Choose Our Ocean Freight
  • Cost-effective for bulk shipments
  • FCL and LCL options available
  • Coverage to major ports worldwide
  • Full customs clearance assistance
  • Refrigerated container options
  • Flexible and reliable scheduling

Road Transport Service

Our road transport network covers East Africa with reliable, door-to-door delivery services. From small parcels to full truckloads, our modern fleet ensures your cargo arrives safely and on time. We offer temperature-controlled vehicles for sensitive goods and real-time GPS tracking for complete peace of mind. Our professional drivers are trained and committed to punctuality and cargo safety.

Why Choose Our Road Transport
  • Door-to-door delivery across East Africa
  • Temperature-controlled vehicles available
  • Real-time GPS tracking
  • Trained and professional drivers
  • Flexible pick-up and delivery scheduling
  • Competitive freight rates

Rail Freight Service

Our rail freight solutions provide an eco-friendly and cost-efficient alternative for bulk cargo and heavy shipments. With access to an extensive rail network, we offer reliable schedules, lower carbon emissions, and significant cost advantages for long-distance transport. Ideal for coal, grain, automobiles, and industrial equipment. Rail transport reduces road congestion while maintaining consistent delivery timelines.

Why Choose Our Rail Freight
  • Eco-friendly, low-emission transport
  • Ideal for heavy and bulk cargo
  • Reliable and consistent schedules
  • Lower carbon footprint vs. road
  • Cost-effective for long-distance routes
  • Extensive national network coverage

Third Party Logistics (3PL)

Let us handle your entire supply chain with our comprehensive 3PL services. We manage inventory, warehousing, order fulfillment, and distribution so you can focus on growing your business. Our advanced systems provide real-time inventory visibility and seamless integration with your e-commerce or ERP platform. From receiving goods at the warehouse to final delivery, we optimize every step of your logistics operation.

Why Choose Our 3PL Services
  • End-to-end supply chain management
  • Real-time inventory tracking
  • E-commerce and ERP integration
  • Order fulfillment and distribution
  • Scalable solutions for all business sizes
  • Dedicated account management

Warehousing & Distribution

Our strategically located distribution centers offer flexible storage solutions with modern infrastructure. Features include 24/7 security, climate-controlled zones, digital inventory management systems, and value-added services such as labeling, kitting, and cross-docking. Scale your storage capacity as your business grows with no long-term commitment required. We provide pick, pack, and ship services for businesses of all sizes.

Why Choose Our Warehousing
  • 24/7 security monitoring
  • Climate-controlled storage zones
  • Digital inventory management system
  • Pick, pack, and ship services
  • Flexible, no long-term contracts
  • Strategically located facilities
