Air Freight Service

Our air freight service delivers your time-sensitive cargo with unmatched speed and reliability. With partnerships across major global airlines and dedicated cargo carriers, we ensure your shipments reach any destination worldwide within 24-72 hours. Perfect for urgent documents, high-value goods, and perishable items requiring rapid transport. Our real-time tracking system keeps you informed at every stage of the journey.

Why Choose Our Air Freight
  • 24-72 hour delivery worldwide
  • Real-time tracking and updates
  • Special handling for perishable goods
  • Customs clearance assistance
  • Competitive pricing for urgent shipments
  • Secure packaging for high-value items

Ocean Freight Service

Our ocean freight solutions offer cost-effective transport for bulk cargo, heavy machinery, and containerized goods. We handle FCL (Full Container Load) and LCL (Less than Container Load) shipments to major ports worldwide. With flexible scheduling and comprehensive customs clearance support, we make international sea trade simple and efficient. Choose from standard, expedited, or refrigerated container options.

Why Choose Our Ocean Freight
  • Cost-effective for bulk shipments
  • FCL and LCL options available
  • Major ports worldwide
  • Customs clearance assistance
  • Refrigerated containers
  • Flexible scheduling

Road Transport Service

Our road transport network covers the entire country with reliable, door-to-door delivery services. From small parcels to full truckloads, our modern fleet ensures your cargo arrives safely and on time. We offer temperature-controlled vehicles for sensitive goods and real-time tracking for complete peace of mind. Our drivers are trained professionals committed to safety and punctuality.

Why Choose Our Road Transport
  • Nationwide door-to-door delivery
  • Temperature-controlled vehicles
  • Real-time GPS tracking
  • Professional drivers
  • Flexible scheduling
  • Competitive rates

Rail Freight Service

Our rail freight solutions provide an eco-friendly alternative for bulk cargo and heavy shipments. With extensive rail network access, we offer reliable schedules, lower carbon emissions, and cost advantages for long-distance transport. Ideal for coal, grain, automobiles, and industrial equipment. Rail transport reduces road congestion while maintaining consistent delivery times.

Why Choose Our Rail Freight
  • Eco-friendly transport
  • Ideal for heavy/bulk cargo
  • Reliable schedules
  • Lower carbon emissions
  • Cost-effective for long distance
  • Extensive network coverage

Third Party Logistics (3PL)

Let us handle your entire supply chain with our comprehensive 3PL services. We manage inventory, warehousing, order fulfillment, and distribution so you can focus on growing your business. Our advanced systems provide real-time inventory visibility and seamless integration with your e-commerce platform. From receiving to shipping, we optimize every step of your logistics.

Why Choose Our 3PL Services
  • End-to-end supply chain management
  • Real-time inventory tracking
  • E-commerce integration
  • Order fulfillment services
  • Scalable solutions
  • Dedicated account manager

Warehousing & Distribution

Our strategically located distribution centers offer flexible storage solutions with modern infrastructure. Features include 24/7 security, climate-controlled zones, inventory management systems, and value-added services like labeling, kitting, and cross-docking. Scale your storage as your business grows with no long-term commitments. We provide pick, pack, and ship services for e-commerce businesses of all sizes.

Why Choose Our Distribution Centers
  • 24/7 security monitoring
  • Climate-controlled zones
  • Inventory management systems
  • Pick, pack, and ship services
  • Flexible storage terms
  • Strategic locations
